# Wearable Memory Assistance: Literature Context

## 1. Microsoft SenseCam vs. SAGE
Microsoft SenseCam was a pioneering wearable device, but it was purely passive and lacked search capability. SAGE advances this paradigm:
*   **Passive vs. Interactive:** SenseCam users had to manually review thousands of images. SAGE indexes memories semantically, enabling conversational, language-driven search.
*   **Monolithic vs. Dual-Buffer:** SenseCam could only capture low-resolution photos without synchronized sound. SAGE uses decoupled dual-buffer capture to stream and align video and audio.

---

## 2. Commercial Smart Pins vs. SAGE
Recent AI-native wearable pins rely entirely on cellular streaming.
*   **Constant Bandwidth Dependency:** Modern smart pins stream audio/video constantly over cellular networks, leading to quick battery drain and network drops. SAGE uses local offline capture and syncs in batches over direct Wi-Fi connections, minimizing power consumption.
*   **User Ownership:** Many smart pins upload data directly to proprietary corporate clouds. SAGE keeps files stored in the user's private Google Drive, giving the user full ownership of their data.
